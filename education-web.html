Getting Started with Green Computing

💻

For Individuals

Enable Power Management: Use sleep modes, screen savers, and auto-shutdown to save energy when devices are idle.

Extend Device Life: Repair and upgrade devices instead of replacing them. Using a laptop for 6 years instead of 3 halves its environmental impact.

Choose Energy Star: Buy ENERGY STAR certified devices for better energy efficiency.

Dispose Responsibly: Recycle electronics properly—never throw them in the trash.

🏢

For Organizations

Conduct Energy Audits: Check your infrastructure for energy waste and improvement opportunities.

Implement Virtualization: Reduce the number of physical servers to save power.

Adopt Cloud Strategically: Choose cloud providers using renewable energy.

Set Policies: Prioritize energy efficiency, durability, and recyclability in procurement, and set measurable sustainability goals.

👨‍💻

For Developers

Write Efficient Code: Optimize algorithms to save energy without compromising performance.

Consider Carbon Cost: Use tools to measure and reduce the environmental impact of software.

Choose Green Hosting: Host applications on renewable-energy-powered servers.

Optimize Data Transfer: Compress data, reduce API calls, and use caching to save energy.

Best Practices by Category

Hardware Management

  • Keep devices 5+ years instead of replacing every 3 years
  • Buy refurbished equipment when possible
  • Use one efficient device instead of multiple gadgets
  • Enable automatic updates to keep systems secure
  • Use cooling pads and external batteries to prolong lifespan
  • Standardize models to simplify maintenance and spare parts

Energy Consumption

  • Turn off monitors after 5–10 minutes of inactivity
  • Enable low-power modes and processor energy-saving settings
  • Lower screen brightness to comfortable levels
  • Unplug chargers when not in use
  • Schedule heavy tasks during off-peak energy hours
  • Close energy-intensive apps when not needed

Data Centers & Cloud

  • Maintain temperatures around 75–80°F to save cooling energy
  • Use hot aisle/cold aisle layouts for airflow efficiency
  • Use outside air cooling when possible
  • Virtualize and consolidate servers
  • Archive or delete unused data to reduce storage
  • Choose cloud regions powered by renewable energy

Software & Applications

  • Close unnecessary tabs and background programs
  • Disable auto-play videos and animations
  • Use lightweight software alternatives
  • Optimize database queries for efficiency
  • Implement lazy loading for web content
  • Compress files and media before saving or sharing
