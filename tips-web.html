Eco-Friendly Tech Tips

Simple Actions, Big Impact

Practical tips and solutions you can use daily to be a greener computer user.

Daily Green Computing Habits

💻

Power Management

Enable Sleep Mode: Set your computer to sleep after 10-15 minutes of inactivity.

Screen Brightness: Reduce your screen brightness to save energy.

Unplug Devices: Remove chargers when not in use to avoid wasting power.

Shut Down Daily: Turn off your computer when you are done for the day.

🌐

Smart Browsing

Close Extra Tabs: Too many open tabs use more memory and eenrgy.

Use Ad Blockers: Reduce unnecessary data loading and usage.

Limit Streaming Quality: Choose in 720p instead of 4K when possible.

Dark Mode: Helps reduce energy use, especially on screens.

📧

Digital Cleanup

Delete Old Emails: Remove unused emails and delete old messages.

Clear Cache Regularly: Remove temporary files and browser cache.

Organize Files: Delete duplicates and unused files.

Uninstall Unused Apps: Uninstall software you no longer use to save resources.

Hardware & Device Care

🔋

Battery Health

Charge Smart: Keep battery between 20-80% for longer lifespan.

Avoid Overcharging: Unplug when fully charged to prevent battery degradation.

Battery Saver Mode: Enable power-saving features when not plugged in.

Temperature Control: Keep devices cool to extend battery life.

🛠️

Maintenance & Repair

Regular Cleaning: Dust off vents and keyboards to prevent overheating.

Software Updates: Install updates to keeps your device running smoothly.

Repair First: Fix devices instead of replacing them when possible.

Upgrade Components: Add RAM or storage to extend device life.

♻️

Extend Device Life

Use Protection: Cases and screen protectors prevent damage.

Proper Storage: Store devices in cool and dry places.

Handle with Care: Avoid physical damage and liquid exposure.

Regular Backups: Protect data to avoid panic replacements.

Conscious Tech Purchasing

🌟

Before You Buy

Ask First Do you really need a new device.

Choose Refurbished: Certified refurbished devices cost less and reduce waste.

Check Quality: Pick devices that are durable and long-lasting.

Support Green Brands: Support companies with strong sustainability commitments.

⚡

Energy Efficiency

Energy-Saving Labels: Look for energy-efficient certifications.

Compare Energy Use: Check power ratings before buying.

Buy What You Need: Don't buy more power than you need.

Efficient Displays: Choose energy-efficient screen technology.

🔄

End-of-Life Planning

Easy Repair: Choose devices that can be easily repaired.

Upgradable Design: Look for upgradeable components.

Recycling Programs: Buy from brands with take-back programs.

Resale or Donate: Consider future resale or donation options.
